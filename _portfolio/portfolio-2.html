" collection: portfolio --- ## 🌍 Project Overview This assessment supports an Afforestation, Reforestation, and Revegetation (ARR) project funded by the Global Environment Facility and implemented by Form International. The objective is to identify and quantify restorable land within flood-affected landscapes in alignment with the Verra ARR methodology. By focusing on hydrologically dynamic areas, the project aims to unlock restoration potential while ensuring environmental integrity and carbon accounting compliance. Flooded areas were delineated using Sentinel-1 SAR imagery combined with historical hydrological datasets to capture both seasonal and permanent inundation patterns. This approach enables clear differentiation between restorable land and ineligible water bodies such as permanent rivers, lakes, and wetlands. A conservative mapping strategy was applied to avoid overestimation and ensure compliance with ARR methodological requirements. The resulting spatial outputs provide a robust basis for defining project boundaries and estimating eligible restoration hectares. These results also support decision-making related to species selection, planting design, and long-term monitoring by identifying areas with suitable hydrological conditions. --- ## 🛰️ Methodology The flood mapping workflow was implemented in Google Earth Engine (GEE) using Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) data, which enables reliable surface water detection under cloud-free and all-weather conditions. Image collections were filtered by acquisition mode (IW), polarization (VV and VH), temporal range, and area of interest. To improve signal consistency and reduce noise, imagery was separated into ascending and descending orbits and temporally averaged. These were then combined into seasonal composites representing four annual periods (2021–2024). This ensured consistent multi-temporal analysis across years. Flood detection was performed using a threshold-based classification on the VV polarization band. Pixels with backscatter values lower than -10 dB were classified as flooded areas, exploiting the low radar return of open water surfaces. Binary flood masks were generated for each year and combined into a multi-band dataset for temporal analysis. Outputs were visualized in GEE and exported as GeoTIFF files at 10-meter resolution. Additionally, summary statistics were derived by calculating flooded pixel counts, supporting quantitative assessment of flood dynamics for ARR eligibility. --- ## 💻 GEE Implementation ```javascript // Function to process Sentinel-1 data for any time period function processSeason(startDate, endDate, seasonName) { var s1 = ee.ImageCollection('COPERNICUS/S1_GRD') .filter(ee.Filter.eq('instrumentMode', 'IW')) .filter(ee.Filter.listContains('transmitterReceiverPolarisation', 'VV')) .filter(ee.Filter.listContains('transmitterReceiverPolarisation', 'VH')) .filterDate(startDate, endDate) .filterBounds(roi);
