<p class="hero__lead">My interest in forests began when I was seven years old, during a flight to
Kalimantan with my mother. From the aircraft window, I remember seeing a striking boundary
between intact tropical rainforest and land that had already been cleared. At that age I did
not yet understand the science behind it, but it was probably the first time I understood
what deforestation looked like in the real world.</p>

<p class="hero__lead">That image stayed with me. It gradually developed into a broader interest
in forests, environmental change, and the diversity of flora and fauna they support. I later
realised that understanding landscapes at this scale requires more than field observation
alone. Over the past five years, this has led me to work with remote sensing, geospatial
analysis, machine learning and deep learning to study forest change and structure in ways that
are more systematic, spatially explicit and measurable.</p>
