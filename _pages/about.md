Deforestation is one of the most pressing challenges of the 21st century. I am intrigued by how the dynamics of vegetation cover affect environmental quality. Moreover, what is the impact of disturbances (i.e., forest conversion, climate change, fire) on the ecosystem? To address these questions, I use an integration of remote sensing technologies and field measurements to describe these phenomena spatially and temporally.
Currently, I serve as a GIS and Forestry consultant at Form International. My role primarily focus on geospatial analyses, such as land-use change and deforestation mapping, and carbon stock modelling to ensure compliance for carbon credit projects under different carbon standards (i.e., Verra and Gold Standard). Additionally, I advise clients in evaluating nature-based credit projects, especially the technical components.

👨🏻‍🔬 Professional Experience

Currently, I am working as a GIS and Forestry Consultant at Form International, where I apply machine learning and remote sensing techniques to support land use change analysis, deforestation monitoring, and carbon accounting for REDD+ and ARR projects. I contribute to spatial analysis, biomass modelling, and Project Design Document (PDD) development across projects in Africa, Asia, and Latin America.

Previously, I worked as a Remote Sensing Intern at Acorn Rabobank, focusing on aboveground biomass estimation using multi-sensor SAR data (Sentinel-1 and ALOS PALSAR-2). I evaluated speckle filtering techniques and developed regression and Random Forest models for low-biomass environments.

During my earlier experience as a Research Assistant at Universitas Gadjah Mada, I contributed to large-scale peatland studies, including long-term land use change analysis and peat depth estimation, supporting research published in Soil Security.

Additionally, I have professional field experience as a Mapping Surveyor at Ministry of Agriculture Indonesia, where I conducted large-scale spatial data collection and mapping of plantation areas using mobile GIS tools.

🌍 Projects and Applied Research

I have extensive experience working on nature-based solutions and carbon projects, including REDD+ and ARR initiatives under Verra methodologies (e.g., VM0007, VM0047). My work includes:

Land use change and deforestation modelling using satellite time series
Carbon stock and biomass estimation
Spatial database management and geospatial workflow development
Time series analysis using platforms such as Google Earth Engine

Selected projects include forest landscape restoration in the Amazon Basin, REDD+ implementation in the Republic of Congo, and afforestation initiatives in Uganda.

📜 Research and Publications

I have contributed to multiple peer-reviewed publications and conference proceedings in remote sensing and geospatial analysis. My research focuses on:

Aboveground biomass estimation using SAR, LiDAR, and optical data
Land use/land cover mapping and accuracy assessment
Tropical peatland monitoring and environmental modelling

I have presented my work at several international conferences, including geoinformation science symposia and remote sensing forums.

🔬 Independent Research and Technical Development
