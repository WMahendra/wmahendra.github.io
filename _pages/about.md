<p class="hero__lead">My interest in forests and natures began when I was seven years old, during a flight to
Kalimantan island with my mother. From the aircraft window, I remember seeing a striking boundary
between intact tropical rainforest and land that had already been cleared. At that age I was not aware the science behind it, but it was probably the first time I understood
what deforestation looked like in the real world.</p>

<p class="hero__lead">That image stayed with me till now and it gradually developed into a broader interest
in forests, environmental, and the diversity of flora and fauna they support. I later
realised that understanding landscapes at this scale requires more than field observation
alone. Over the past five years, this has led me to work with remote sensing, geospatial
analysis, machine learning to study forest change and structure in ways that
are more systematic, spatially explicit and measurable. I want to work hard to protect trees, as they have been protecting our planet for hundreds of millions of years.
</p>
