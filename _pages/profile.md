Deforestation is one of the most pressing challenges of the 21st century. I am intrigued by how the dynamics of vegetation cover affect environmental quality. Moreover, what is the impact of disturbances (i.e., forest conversion, climate change, fire) on the ecosystem? To address these questions, I use an integration of remote sensing technologies and field measurements to describe these phenomena spatially and temporally.
Currently, I serve as a GIS and Forestry consultant at Form International. My role primarily focus on geospatial analyses, such as land-use change and deforestation mapping, and carbon stock modelling to ensure compliance for carbon credit projects under different carbon standards (i.e., Verra and Gold Standard). Additionally, I advise clients in evaluating nature-based credit projects, especially the technical components.

🛰️ Using Remote Sensing, GIS, and AI to Address Global Challenges

I apply a combination of satellite data, geospatial analysis, and machine learning to tackle key environmental problems:

🌳 Deforestation Monitoring
Using time-series satellite imagery (e.g., Sentinel, Landsat), I detect and map forest loss in near real-time. AI-driven classification models help distinguish between natural changes and human-driven deforestation, improving accuracy and scalability.

🌫️ Carbon Loss and Climate Impact
Through biomass modelling and carbon stock estimation, I quantify how much carbon is stored in forests—and how much is released when forests are degraded or cleared. These insights directly support climate mitigation efforts and carbon credit projects (e.g., REDD+, ARR).

🦜 Biodiversity and Ecosystem Health
Forest structure and vegetation indices derived from remote sensing serve as proxies for biodiversity. By monitoring habitat fragmentation and ecosystem integrity, I help identify areas at risk and support conservation planning.

👩‍🌾 Livelihoods and Sustainable Land Use
Forests are deeply connected to local communities. My work integrates spatial analysis with socio-environmental context to assess how land-use changes affect livelihoods, enabling better decision-making for sustainable development and nature-based solutions.

🤖 From Data to Decisions

My approach bridges data and impact:

Remote sensing provides continuous, large-scale environmental observations
GIS enables spatial analysis and integration of diverse datasets
AI and machine learning extract patterns, improve predictions, and automate workflows

Together, these tools transform raw satellite data into meaningful insights that support:

Climate action and carbon accounting
Biodiversity conservation strategies
Sustainable land-use planning
Evidence-based policy and project development
🌱 Impact

Through this integrated approach, I contribute to advancing nature-based solutions that balance environmental protection with human well-being—helping ensure that forests continue to support climate stability, biodiversity, and resilient livelihoods.
