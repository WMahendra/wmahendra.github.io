## Objective

One or two sentences: what this explores, stated concretely.

## Why I explored it

The reason it was worth the time - a gap in a workflow, a method worth testing, a
question left over from other work. Keep it to a couple of sentences.

## Data

- Sensor / dataset and the time period
- Study area
- Anything else needed to reproduce it

## Technical approach

The steps, briefly. Bullets are fine - this does not need to read like a paper.

- What actually came out of it, including results that were inconclusive
- Keep every number traceable to something you ran
